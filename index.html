Master
logo

Our most recognisable asset.

The master logo combines the stag, the Borjomi valley scene, the wordmark in Latin and Georgian script, our founding year and the descriptor. Use it on packaging and hero moments.

Always reproduce it from the master artwork. Never redraw, rearrange or recolour its parts.

Preferred: on Light Mint
On Borjomi Navy

Don’t rotate
Don’t place on off‑brand colours

Wordmark

For small sizes and secondary use.

Use the wordmark where the master logo would be too detailed: digital headers, merchandise, small print. It appears in Borjomi Navy or white only.

Keep clear space equal to the height of the “O” on all sides.

Stag

The guardian of the springs.

The red stag is our emblem. Use it alone as a signature on caps, social avatars and small brand moments. It is always Borjomi Red with a white keyline.

The stag always faces left and stands upright. Don’t crop through its antlers.

On Light Mint
On Teal
On Navy

Scene

Where our water comes from.

The scene shows the Borjomi valley: Caucasus peaks, the historic pavilion of the mineral water park and the spring fountain, with the stag at its heart.

Use it on white or Light Mint backgrounds so the mountains stay readable.

Pattern

Inspired by bubbles and old ironwork.

The line pattern of interlocking circles echoes rising mineral bubbles and the ornament of the Borjomi park pavilions.

Use it in white on Teal or Navy as a background texture. Never place it behind the master logo.
